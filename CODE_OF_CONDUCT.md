> [!IMPORTANT]
> This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and every other EliteGitBot community space.

## 🤝 Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EliteGitBot a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## ✨ Our standards

### Behavior we encourage

| | Standard | What it looks like |
|:---:|---|---|
| 💬 | Constructive communication | Clear, respectful feedback focused on the work |
| 🤝 | Empathy and collaboration | Welcoming questions and helping others learn |
| ✅ | Accountability | Accepting feedback, correcting mistakes, and apologizing when appropriate |
| 🌍 | Inclusive participation | Respecting different backgrounds, identities, skills, and viewpoints |
| 🛡️ | Responsible disclosure | Protecting private information and reporting vulnerabilities privately |

### Behavior we do not accept

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive conduct
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, private repository content, or undisclosed vulnerabilities
- Any behavior reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community

> [!TIP]
> Disagreement is welcome. Attack the problem, never the person.

---

## 🌐 Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents EliteGitBot in public spaces, events, or direct community communication.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond proportionately to the circumstances.

### Enforcement principles

1. Reports are reviewed privately and impartially.
2. Only the information needed to investigate is shared.
3. Responses are proportional to impact, context, and repeated behavior.
4. Possible actions include clarification, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal.
5. Retaliation against a reporter or participant is prohibited.

> [!WARNING]
> For security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public conduct report.
